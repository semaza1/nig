Ibikora

Byose mu buryo bumwe

Sisitemu igenewe gufasha abayobozi b'amakimina gucunga imirimo yose nta makosa.

💰

Imigabane

Injiza no gukurikirana imigabane ya buri munyamuryango buri kwezi mu buryo bwizewe.

🏦

Inguzanyo

Gucunga inguzanyo kuva igihe isabwa kugeza iyo yishyuriwe, harimo na abishingira.

📊

Inyungu (Reducing Balance)

Kubara inyungu z'inguzanyo hashingiwe ku mwenda usigaye buri kwezi nta makosa.

📋

Transactions

Kuzenguruka amasoko yose — contribution, withdrawal, loan payment, expense — hamwe.

🏠

Assets

Gukurikirana imutungo y'ikimina — impamyabuzima, itangazo n'igiciro cyayo.

📈

Raporo & Dashboard

Raporo z'ingenzi zikubiyemo imibare yose y'ikimina mu buryo bworoshye gusobanukirwa.

Abakoresha

Ibyiciro by'abakoresha

Buri wese afite inshingano ze zihariye mu gucunga ikimina.

Umuyobozi

Admin

  • Yandika abanyamuryango bashya
  • Yinjiza imigabane n'inguzanyo
  • Yemeza inguzanyo zisabwa
  • Areba raporo zose z'ikimina
  • Agenzura igenamiterere
Umunyamuryango

Member

  • Atanga umusanzu wa buri kwezi
  • Asaba inguzanyo
  • Areba imigabane ye
  • Yishyura inguzanyo ze
  • Ashobora kuba umushingira
Utari umunyamuryango

Non-Member

  • Asaba inguzanyo gusa
  • Agomba abishingira 2
  • Yishyura inyungu mbere
  • Areba inguzanyo ze

Uburyo

Gutangira biraryoshye

Intambwe enye gusa kugira ngo utangire gucunga ikimina yawe.

1

Fungura Konti

Iyandikishe na email kandi ushyire amakuru y'ikimina yawe.

2

Ongera Abanyamuryango

Injiza abanyamuryango bose n'amakuru yabo mu sisitemu.

3

Injiza Transactions

Tandika kuzinjiza imigabane, inguzanyo n'amasoko yose.

4

Reba Raporo

Kureba imiterere y'ikimina kuri dashboard igenwa n'amakuru.

Tangira ubu

Gutegura ikimina yawe?

Injira mu sisitemu cyangwa fungura konti nshya. Ikoranabuhanga riragufasha gucunga byose nta ngorane.
